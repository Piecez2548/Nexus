# Nexus Terms of Use — Draft Scope for Thai Legal Review

**Status:** internal drafting instructions only. This is not a user contract and must not be represented as legal approval.

## Required parties and acceptance record

Identify the operator's legal name, address/contact, eligible territories, minimum age, effective date, version and governing language. Present final terms before account creation with an unticked acceptance control separate from optional consent. Store the accepted version, timestamp and account identifier. Make terms available before login and in settings; define material-change notice and renewed acceptance.

## Service and advice boundaries

Describe Nexus All, Main, public Tools, mobile, optional sync/encryption/AI and separately hosted DataLens. Nexus and DataLens are information tools, not a bank, broker, accountant, insurer, fiduciary, tax/legal/investment adviser or licensed auditor. Calculations, OCR, inferred types, quality scores, recommendations and AI output may be incomplete or wrong. Users remain responsible for source authority, reconciliation, professional review and decisions.

An DataLens approval event records an authorized user's workflow action. It is not an audit opinion, legal approval, data certification or assurance engagement. Public UI and exported reports must say so beside the approval action.

## Organizational and third-party data

Require users to have authority and a lawful basis for data they enter, scan, sync or upload. Organizational DataLens use needs controller/processor terms covering documented instructions, confidentiality, subprocessors, security, deletion/return, rights assistance and incident cooperation; consumer terms alone are insufficient.

## Account, privacy and security

Cover credential/MFA/device responsibilities, prohibited account sharing, compromise notification, suspension, deletion, backup and recovery. Explain that the Main PIN is device-local, optional encryption has separate behavior and export backups are plaintext. Incorporate the final privacy notice by link without using terms acceptance as blanket consent.

## Ownership and acceptable use

Define ownership of user data, the narrow processing licence, software/content rights, open-source notices and export/report ownership. Prohibit unlawful surveillance, credential collection, infringement, malware, evasion of access controls and restricted-data processing without authority.

## Commercial, liability and termination terms

If payment is introduced, specify price, tax, renewal, cancellation, refund and trial rules before charging. Use warranty/liability language Thai counsel confirms is enforceable; do not exclude liability that law does not permit excluding. State termination grounds, notice/appeal, export opportunity, deletion timetable and surviving provisions. Distinguish device deletion, cloud deletion, provider logs and user-created copies.

## Claims that must not be made

Do not promise guaranteed synchronization/recovery, continuous availability, live prices, guaranteed outcomes, enterprise SLA, “bank-grade” security or regulatory certification unless there is current evidence and an enforceable commitment.

## Release gate

Do not publish until operator/service facts, privacy notice, acceptance evidence, deletion workflow, support route, organizational DataLens terms, consumer/commercial rules, governing law/dispute route and liability language are complete and verified in production.
