# Nexus User Research Plan

## Objective

Determine whether Thai salaried workers and freelancers repeatedly use Nexus to understand daily money, and which capability they would pay for. Research must discover behavior; it must not be presented as customer validation until sessions are completed.

## Recruitment

Recruit 15–30 participants across variable and stable income, beginner and experienced financial knowledge, Android and desktop use, and users who currently rely on spreadsheets, bank apps, or no tracking system. Do not label participants by social class. Record income only as an optional range when needed for analysis.

## Interview flow

1. Ask how the participant currently answers “Can I safely spend this week?”
2. Observe their current process without showing Nexus.
3. Give them a fresh Nexus installation and ask them to add or import realistic sample data.
4. Ask them to find cash flow, the next bill, budget risk, and savings progress without guidance.
5. Let them choose Simple or Pro and explain what they expect each to contain.
6. Test export, restore, lock, and account recovery using non-sensitive sample data.
7. Show candidate paid packages without leading language; record rejection reasons.

## Evidence capture

Record task completion, time, errors, points of confusion, chosen mode, trust concerns, return behavior, and exact willingness-to-pay response. Never collect real PINs, passwords, Vault entries, bank credentials, or unredacted financial statements.

## Decision rules

- Continue the finance-first direction when activation and repeat-use thresholds in `COMMERCIAL_READINESS.md` are met and interviews identify a repeated financial job.
- Revise onboarding or Simple mode when users succeed only with moderator help.
- Do not build organization features from executive interest alone; require a repeated company workflow, a budget owner, and willingness to run a pilot.
- Stop or narrow a proposed integration when its privacy, licensing, or support cost exceeds demonstrated user value.

## Pilot checklist

- Use sample data for the moderated session.
- Obtain explicit consent for notes or recordings.
- Provide a deletion contact and remove research records on request.
- Separate product telemetry from interview identity.
- Review findings by behavior and evidence, not participant status or income stereotype.
