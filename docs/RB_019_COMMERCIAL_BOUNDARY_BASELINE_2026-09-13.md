# RB-019 — Commercial boundary baseline (2026-09-13)

The current product documents define commercial hypotheses only. No paid package, price, refund policy or customer SLA was approved or published.

## Current boundary

- Nexus is positioned as a personal finance-first tool for Thai users; it must not be marketed as corporate accounting or investment advice.
- Candidate paid value is encrypted multi-device sync, managed backup/recovery, advanced reports, licensed market data and household sharing. These remain hypotheses.
- Organization/DataLens use requires separate controller/processor, DPA, support and architecture decisions.
- Existing deployments remain demo-ready with synthetic data; launch claims, revenue, conversion, retention and willingness-to-pay results are not asserted.

Infrastructure, support, license, payment and provider costs have not been measured against an approved offer. RB-019 remains **In Progress** pending CEO/Product/Finance approval, user research and Legal review of the resulting offer terms.
